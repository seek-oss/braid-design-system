**Avatar:** Add component

A rounded-square identity mark for a person or company. Shows an image, initials from `name`, a provided `icon`, or `IconProfile`. A loaded `imageUrl` ignores `icon`. Sizes: `xsmall` 24, `small` 32, `medium` 40, `standard` 48, `large` 64, `xlarge` 80, `xxlarge` 96.

**EXAMPLE USAGE:**

```jsx
<Avatar name="Leia Organa" />
<Avatar icon={<IconCompany />} />
```
